# Omega spell icons

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using the supplied flower and an existing Explosion A frame as references. Transparent output was preserved and reduced to 64×64 with nearest-neighbor sampling for the Minecraft HUD.

Final assets:
- `src/main/resources/assets/irons_omega_rush/textures/gui/spell_icons/omega_form.png`
- `src/main/resources/assets/irons_omega_rush/textures/gui/spell_icons/omega_rush.png`

Iron's native `AbstractSpell.getSpellIconResource()` requests `textures/gui/spell_icons/<spell name>.png`. Earlier assets were in `textures/spell/`, which the native HUD did not load.

## Form prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Minecraft Iron's Spells spell-wheel/HUD icon for Omega Form. Use the attached flower sprite as the recognizable subject reference. Create ONE isolated square icon on a truly transparent background: a cheerful golden-yellow six-petaled flower, pale cream round face, two simple brown eyes and small friendly smile, short vivid green stem. Large clear flower head occupies most of the square, with a restrained thin six-color rainbow energy halo behind it to suggest transformation. Crisp chunky pixel art on a logical 64x64 grid, hard pixel edges, strong silhouette readable at 16x16 HUD size, compact limited shading, dark pixel outline. Center composition with about 8% transparent margin. Remove the reference's white scribble/swoosh, black background, and soil platform. No text, letters, UI, border box, watermark, soft blur, or realistic rendering.

## Rush prompt

Create ONE isolated square icon on a genuinely transparent background. A powerful compact prismatic explosion: brilliant ivory-white starburst core, chunky six-hue rainbow blast rim, a bold angled orbital shock ring around the burst matching the reference's planet-like explosion shape, and three short colorful backward streaks suggesting rushing forward. Crisp blocky pixel-art game sprite on a logical 64x64 grid; strong simple silhouette and contrast so it remains recognizable at tiny 16x16 HUD scale. Match the cheerful saturated rainbow pixel-art treatment of a golden flower transformation icon. Centered with about 8% transparent margin. Stylish and energetic, not a conventional orange fireball. No flower or face in this icon. No text, letters, border box, UI background, watermark, gradients made of soft blur, or photorealistic details.
